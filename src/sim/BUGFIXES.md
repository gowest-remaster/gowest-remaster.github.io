The 1996 game had bugs that most players never noticed. Remastered fixes them. Classic mode keeps
every one of them on purpose, so it plays exactly like the original.

## Small rule changes for a fairer game (3)

- Wages, O'Grady's wagon and livestock prices are set once a month, so walking out and back in no longer changes them.
- Looking around town can no longer change your luck: weather and disasters follow only your real decisions.
- The trivia card waits for you until the end of the month instead of vanishing when you walk away.
